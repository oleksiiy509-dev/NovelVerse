# NovelVerse user guide

Use **Catalog** to discover novels, **Library** to save titles, and **Reader** to continue chapters. Production administrators can assign character voices in Voice Studio, direct scenes, generate narration and sound design, mix tracks, assemble an audiobook, and export the result.

The **Production Control** page runs the complete eight-stage pipeline. A job may be stopped, resumed, or cancelled. NovelVerse saves recovery checkpoints automatically. If speech fails, confirm the local voice worker and Piper model are running, then use **Diagnostics → Run health check**. Export the safe report when requesting support; secrets are redacted.

Settings are grouped and searchable. Use **Export settings** before major changes, **Import settings** to restore a JSON backup, or **Reset to defaults** to recover recommended behavior. Offline reading remains available for previously cached content; synthesis and cloud-backed actions require their respective services.
